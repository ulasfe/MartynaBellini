About the artist

Martyna
Bellini

Acoustic guitarist and songwriter from Warsaw, Poland — crafting intimate songs that feel like the quiet of nature.

🎸

Martyna Bellini began playing guitar at the age of 12, drawn to the warmth and intimacy of acoustic music. Influenced by the sounds of the Polish countryside and the quiet of forest mornings, her compositions are deeply rooted in nature and personal experience.

Her style blends fingerstyle technique with delicate vocal harmonies, creating a sound that is both modern and timeless. Each song is a small story — honest, unfiltered, and alive.

Martyna performs regularly in Warsaw and across Poland, and her music is available on Spotify and YouTube. She is open to collaborations, live session requests, and bookings.
