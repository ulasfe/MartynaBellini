About the artist

Martyna
Bellini

Acoustic guitarist and songwriter from Wroclaw, Poland — crafting intimate songs that feel like the quiet of nature.

🎸

Martyna Bellini is a singer, songwriter, and live performer based in Wrocław, whose music blends emotional storytelling with atmospheric acoustic soundscapes and heartfelt vocals. Her musical journey began at the age of eight when she started learning piano and performing her first concerts as a young musician, developing a deep connection to music from an early age.

At the age of thirteen, she discovered the guitar — an instrument that soon became an essential part of her artistic expression and songwriting. Since 2013, the singer has been performing publicly, gradually shaping her own distinctive style rooted in authenticity, emotion, and intimate connection with audiences.

She holds degrees in Musicology and Indology, combining musical knowledge with a deep interest in languages, cultures, and artistic traditions from around the world. This multicultural influence can also be heard in her performances, which often include acoustic cover songs in multiple languages, including English, Polish, Italian, Hindi, Turkish, Arabic, and German.

Today, she performs acoustic concerts singing and playing guitar, creating warm and atmospheric live sets filled with emotion and sincerity. She regularly performs at restaurants, cafés, private venues, cultural spaces, and artistic events in Wrocław, as well as selected concerts across Europe, bringing a personal and intimate atmosphere to every performance.

Alongside her live work, the artist has released original music on Spotify. While she has currently released one original song, she continues to work on new music and hopes to release more singles and full albums in the future, further developing her artistic journey and musical identity.

Inspired by personal experiences, travel, and emotion, her music is honest, atmospheric, and deeply human — connecting with listeners through vulnerability, warmth, and authentic expression. She is also open to artistic collaborations, live performance opportunities, and creative musical projects.
